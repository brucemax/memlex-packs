## Memlex Premium

Premium is sold in these forms, with the price shown by the store before you pay (the Premium screen shows the ones on sale):

- **Yearly.** An auto-renewing subscription for one year. It renews every year at the same price until you cancel it.

  Play or the App Store); Premium then stays active until the end of the year you paid for.
- **Monthly.** The same as yearly, renewing every month.
- **Lifetime.** A one-time purchase with no renewal.

Payments, renewals, cancellations and refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store under their rules; the
developer does not see or store your payment details. Purchases are tied to your store account, not to an account in
the app: use "Restore purchases" on the Premium screen after reinstalling or on a new device with the same store
account.

If the app stops offering a Premium feature you paid for, the remaining Premium features stay available to you.
